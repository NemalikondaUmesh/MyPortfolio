About Hyderabad

Hyderabad is one of India's most historic and rapidly growing cities. Popularly called the "City of Pearls", Hyderabad combines royal heritage with modern urban lifestyle. The city is famous for Charminar, Golconda Fort, Tank Bund, Ramoji Film City, Salar Jung Museum, and Birla Mandir. Hyderabad is also world-famous for biryani, shopping markets, tourism experiences, and architectural wonders.
